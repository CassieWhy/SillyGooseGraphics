Meet Cassie

I’ve always believed art is about more than what you make. It’s about the feeling, the story, and the experience of bringing an idea to life together.

What started as passion and a little creative chaos grew into a career built on years of learning, experimenting, and showing up. I love turning sparks of ideas into bold, thoughtful designs, paintings, murals, and visual stories.

Working with clients is one of my favorite parts of what I do. Whether you have a clear vision or just a “hear me out” moment, I care about making the process smooth, collaborative, and actually fun.

Deadlines matter, details matter, and good vibes matter.

Let’s make something unforgettable.
